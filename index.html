03 · Vision Transformer

Read in patches, understood in context.

Pigment uses ViT-B/16. It divides a photo into 16×16-pixel patches and learns how they relate to one another, then outputs a probability for each of two classes.

  • Fine-tuned on the Kaggle vitiligo image dataset
  • Evaluated with standard classification metrics
  • No accuracy or clinical-validation claims are made here

How it works

From photograph to informational result.

  1. 1

    Upload or capture

    Choose an image from your device or take one with your camera.

  2. 2

    Preprocessing

    The image is resized to 224×224 and normalized by the ViT image processor.

  3. 3

    Model inference

    The fine-tuned ViT-B/16 model scores the image for each class.

  4. 4

    Informational result

    You see the predicted class and per-class confidence, nothing more.

Limitations: lighting, skin tone, image quality and background can all change the result. A prediction is not a diagnosis. Please consult a qualified dermatologist about any skin concern.
